Create a new Spotify app

This usually means the app was deleted in the Spotify dashboard. Make a new one and connect through it. Your routines and history stay.

  1. Create an app in the dashboard (Spotify Premium is required).
  2. Add the redirect URL and your account, then paste the new client ID.
  3. Connect Spotify with the same account as before.
Start setup again
