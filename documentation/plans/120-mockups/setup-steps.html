Step 1 · Your Spotify app

Create a Spotify app

Raccoon runs through your own Spotify developer app. It's free and stays private to you. Setup takes about ten minutes, and we'll walk you through every click.

You need Spotify Premium. Spotify only lets Premium accounts own a developer app, and allows one per person. If you already have one you no longer use, delete it in the dashboard first.
  1. Open the dashboard below, sign in with your Spotify account and choose Create app.
  2. Give it any name and description.
  3. Under Which API/SDKs, tick Web API.
  4. Agree to Spotify's terms and Save.

Step 2 · Your Spotify app

Tell Spotify where to send you back

Add the redirect URL

In your app, choose Settings, then Edit, and paste this under Redirect URIs, then save.

https://raccoon.fm/callback
Copy

If it's missing or not exactly this, Spotify stops on its own error page and never sends you back to Raccoon. If that happens, return here and check it was saved exactly.

Allow your Spotify account

Under User Management, add the name and email of the Spotify account you'll connect.

Step 3 · Your Spotify app

Paste your app's client ID

It's on your app's Settings page. Only the client ID: never your client secret.

32 characters, letters and numbers
Raccoon never asks for your client secret.
Save client ID
If it isn't a client ID
That doesn't look like a client ID. It's 32 letters and numbers, with no spaces. Check you didn't copy the client secret or a link.
If it's saved
Client ID saved. One step left: connecting is what checks it.
If the ID is wrong but looks right
Spotify shows a page that says "INVALID_CLIENT". You won't be sent back to Raccoon from it. Come back here, fix the client ID and try again.
